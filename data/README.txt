hedgerow_map_sh.dat - hedgerow map of Schleswig-Holstein for XP12 Forest Tweak

Based on data of: LLUR SH, Biotopkartierung SH4 - Alleen, Knicks, Feldhecken und Baumreihen (2023)
https://opendata.schleswig-holstein.de/dataset/sh4-biotopkartierung-der-alleen-knicks-feldhecken-und-baumreihen
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Changes: only the line geometry and its type (Knick, Feldhecke, Baumreihe, Allee) are kept, coordinates
(ETRS89 / UTM 32, EPSG 4647) rounded to 0.1 m and stored in a compact gzip format. The tool turns them into
X-Plane forest strips and tree rows on the user's computer.
